Privacy

BuhTig keeps only what it needs to show you dashboards, and to remember you once you sign in. It uses no analytics, trackers, ads or scripts from other sites.

Cookies

BuhTig sets two cookies, and only when you sign in. Signing in can't work without them.

  • buhtig_session keeps you signed in. It lasts until you sign out or your session ends.
  • buhtig_sign_in lets BuhTig check that it's you who comes back from GitHub when you sign in. It lasts 10 minutes at most, and goes as soon as you're back.

BuhTig also notes in your browser's session storage that it has played its wordmark's animation, so that it plays once per visit. The note never leaves your browser, which forgets it when you close the tab.

If you turn off the / shortcut on the About page, BuhTig notes that in your browser's local storage, so that it stays off. That note never leaves your browser either. It goes when you turn the shortcut back on, or clear this site's data.

If you don't sign in

  • Request logs. For each request, the method, the path, the status and how long BuhTig took to answer. Not your IP address, cookies or query strings. Whoever runs this BuhTig decides how long its logs are kept.
  • Counts. GitHub limits how much BuhTig may ask of it, so BuhTig counts how many dashboards each visitor loads from GitHub in an hour. So that no one can guess at sign-ins or tokens, it also counts how often each visitor tries them in a minute. It keeps the counts in memory only, under a hash of your IP address rather than the address itself, and forgets them once the hour or minute is over, or when it restarts.

That is all BuhTig keeps when you don't sign in.

If you sign in

  • Your account. Your GitHub user ID, login, display name and avatar's address, and when you first and last signed in, so that BuhTig knows who you are. Not your email address. BuhTig keeps it until you delete your data.
  • Your sessions. For each browser you're signed in on, when you signed in and last used BuhTig, and a hash of the session's ID rather than the ID itself. A session ends when you sign out, after 7 days without use, or 30 days after you signed in.
  • Your GitHub access. The access GitHub gave BuhTig when you signed in, which lets BuhTig read only what is public on GitHub. BuhTig keeps it encrypted until you delete your data.
  • Your token. If you save a personal access token, BuhTig keeps it encrypted, to read what it lets you see on GitHub. It also keeps what Settings shows of it: its type, last four characters, scopes or permissions, expiry date, whether GitHub still accepts it, and when BuhTig last checked. It keeps them until you remove or replace the token.
  • What your token read. The GitHub data that BuhTig read with your token, so that it need not ask GitHub every time. Only you are shown it. BuhTig deletes it when you remove or replace the token, and otherwise 30 days after it last read it from GitHub.
  • Your history. The 20 dashboards you viewed last, and when, so that you can go back to them from the home page. You can clear it in Settings.
  • Your pins. Up to 12 dashboards you pinned, in your order. They stay until you unpin them.

Whoever runs this BuhTig may set other periods for sessions and for GitHub data.

GitHub data

BuhTig keeps the public GitHub data that its dashboards show, so that it need not ask GitHub every time. This data is about repositories and organizations, not about you, and BuhTig drops the email addresses that GitHub gives with it, such as commit authors', before keeping it. BuhTig deletes it 30 days after it last read it from GitHub, unless whoever runs it sets another period.

Avatars load straight from GitHub, so GitHub sees your browser ask for them. When you sign in, you do so on GitHub, where GitHub's own privacy statement applies.

Deleting your data

If you've signed in, you can delete everything BuhTig keeps about you at once: go to Settings and choose “Delete all my data”. You can also clear your history, unpin dashboards and remove your token there.

Deleting your data doesn't remove BuhTig from the apps you've authorized on GitHub. You can revoke its access in your GitHub settings. Review your authorized apps on GitHub

If you haven't signed in, nothing BuhTig keeps identifies you, so there is nothing to delete.
